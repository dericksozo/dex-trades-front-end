Powered by SIM IDX

DexTrades showcases streaming swaps indexed in real time by SIM IDX. Every metric on this dashboard is generated directly from on-chain activity fetched from the public Dex Trades API.

All requests are served from cb546b6e70-a19f596caf.idx.sim.io with sub-second ingestion of trades across Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Unichain, and more. The front end is intentionally framework-free – just HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript – to demonstrate how quickly you can visualize on-chain flows without additional tooling.

Want to dig deeper? Explore the source on GitHub and remix this UI with your own analytics or custom styling.
